Boys and Girls Club

2025 Community Center Professional - Austin Architects

During my internship with Austin Architects, I was responsible for producing a series of high-quality visualizations for the firm’s ongoing Boys and Girls Club of Waltham, MA project. I developed the renders using Revit’s native rendering environment, ensuring accurate representation of design intent, materiality, and lighting.

I then refined and enhanced the images through post-processing in Photoshop, adjusting color balance, contrast, and composition to create compelling and client-ready presentations.
