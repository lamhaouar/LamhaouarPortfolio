Resin Architecture 2025

2025 Adaptive Reuse / Community Center

Common Grounds reimagines an existing warehouse as a two-part community resource center focused on natural living, foraging, and local food systems. By reusing the structure, the project reduces environmental impact while preserving local character.

A public-facing shop and plant nursery introduce ethical, community-oriented consumption, while an education and research wing offers hands-on learning through culinary, ecological, and botanical programs. Extended by outdoor vendor spaces and a reforested “living classroom,” the center fosters collective knowledge, ecological care, and community resilience through accessible, place-based learning.
