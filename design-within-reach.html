Design Within Reach

2026 Retail Professional - Interiors Intern at MillerKnoll

As an Interiors Intern at MillerKnoll, I conducted a comprehensive spatial analysis of the existing Design Within Reach retail fleet. The project involved documenting current store environments, developing accurate 3D digital models of the architectural elements and furniture layouts, and producing proposed spatial refreshes. These design interventions were specifically developed to align older retail locations with the updated visual and spatial identity of newer DWR stores.
